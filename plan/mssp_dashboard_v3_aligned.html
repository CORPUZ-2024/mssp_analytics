Module A — key finding

Counties with YoY risk score growth >10% AND expenditure growth exceeding the national trend factor (~3.6% for PY2024) show V28 coding exposure patterns consistent with RADV audit targeting criteria. For ESRD (Broward), negative V28 exposure index signals CKD coding specificity opportunity — Stage 4/5 documentation rewarded under V28. Aged Non-Dual counties (Cook) with high vascular/metabolic HCC exposure face V28 compression regardless of coding quality — document as methodology artifact. Harris excluded from primary ranking due to TEAM_county confound flag.

Module B — key finding

ACOs with RAF above 1.20 systematically exceed benchmark under Track A. V28 adjustment column shows increasing negative skew at higher RAF bands — the V28 version skew is largest exactly where financial pressure is already highest. The 0.86–1.00 band is the analytically interesting case: counties are generating savings but failing to clear MSR, meaning the benchmark methodology penalizes moderate-complexity populations. TEAM episode pricing will face the same dynamic for surgical patients with comorbidities in the 1.01–1.20 RAF range.

Module C — key finding

MSSP-aligned populations show slightly elevated simulated denial rates vs. FFS (8.6% vs. 7.7%), consistent with higher specialty utilization in ACO populations. Field 4 (appeal approval rate) shows the largest gap vs. MA benchmarks — 61% vs. >80% — suggesting either ACO-adjacent populations face more defensible initial denials, or lower propensity to appeal. Field 5 (extended review) has no public benchmark; this is an analytical gap in the CMS-0057-F public reporting schema itself, not a data limitation of this simulation.
